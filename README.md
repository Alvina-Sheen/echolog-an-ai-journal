# EchoLog -- An AI-Powered Journaling Platform

EchoLog is an AI-powered journaling web application designed to make
daily journaling easier, more personal, and more engaging. It helps
users turn simple keywords into meaningful journal entries, record
thoughts using voice input, reflect on their mood, and organize personal
and professional experiences in one place.

## Project Overview

Writing a journal regularly can be difficult when users do not know how
to begin, have limited time to write, or find it hard to express their
thoughts. EchoLog addresses these challenges with guided sentence
generation, voice-to-text input, mood-aware activities, and tools for
organizing everyday tasks and memories.

The platform supports both **personal** and **professional** journaling,
allowing users to record different aspects of their lives in a
structured and convenient way.

## Key Features

### 1. Keyword-Based Journaling

-   Users enter a few keywords describing an event, thought, or
    experience.
-   Users select a preferred tone.
-   EchoLog generates sentence suggestions based on the entered keywords
    and selected tone.
-   Users can choose the sentence that best expresses their thoughts and
    save it as a journal entry.

**Purpose:** Helps users get started with writing and express their
thoughts without having to compose every sentence from scratch.

### 2. Voice-to-Text Journaling

-   Allows users to speak their thoughts instead of typing them.
-   Converts spoken input into text for journaling.

**Purpose:** Makes journal entry more convenient for users who prefer
speaking or want to record thoughts quickly.

### 3. Mood Detection

-   Analyzes journal input to identify signs of a sad or negative mood.
-   Uses the detected mood to support a more interactive journaling
    experience.

**Purpose:** Encourages users to reflect on their emotions. Mood
detection is intended as a supportive feature, not a medical or
mental-health diagnosis.

### 4. Mood-Based Mini-Games and Activities

-   When a negative mood is detected, the platform can trigger a small
    game or activity.
-   The activity offers users an optional, engaging break from writing.

**Purpose:** Adds an interactive element that may help users pause,
reset, and engage with the journaling process in a more positive way. It
is not a substitute for professional support.

### 5. Personal and Professional Journals

-   Provides separate modes for personal and professional entries.
-   Helps users keep private reflections and work-related notes
    organized.

**Purpose:** Allows users to journal about different parts of life
without mixing all entries into one stream.

### 6. Role-Specific Professional Journaling

-   Supports context-specific labels for professional users.
-   For example, a teacher can organize an entry around subjects,
    students, or homework.

**Purpose:** Makes professional journaling more relevant to the user's
role and daily responsibilities.



### 7. To-Do List Management

-   Provides a place to record and manage tasks.

**Purpose:** Brings everyday task organization together with journaling,
helping users keep track of activities and responsibilities.

## How It Works

1.  Choose personal or professional journaling.
2.  Enter journaling method (keyword based journaling/voice to text/normal journaling)
    describing the thought, event, or experience.
4.  Select a preferred tone for keyword-based sentence generation.
5.  Review the generated sentence suggestions.
6.  Select the sentence that best represents the user's thoughts and
    save it to the journal.
7.  Use other features, such as mood-based activities, motivational quotes,
    and to-do lists, as needed.

## Social Impact

EchoLog is designed to make reflective writing more accessible and
approachable. Its potential benefits include:

-   **Lowering the barrier to journaling:** Keyword-based sentence
    suggestions can help people who struggle to find the right words.
-   **Supporting self-reflection:** Regular journaling can give users a
    convenient way to review thoughts, experiences, and emotions.
-   **Improving accessibility and convenience:** Voice-to-text input
    offers an alternative to typing.
-   **Encouraging emotional awareness:** Mood-related features can
    prompt users to notice how they are feeling and offer optional
    activities when negative emotions are detected.
-   **Helping users stay organized:** Personal and professional
    journals, motivatinal quotes and task lists bring several
    everyday tools into one platform.

These are intended benefits; the application does not diagnose
mental-health conditions or replace professional care.
